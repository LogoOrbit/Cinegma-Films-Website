Questions & Answers

TVC & Ad Film Production — Questions

Does Cinegma Films produce TV commercials for broadcast?

Yes. We produce TVCs in 15, 30 and 60-second cuts and deliver broadcast-standard 4K masters, along with digital and social versions of the same campaign.

Can you work from an agency storyboard, or do you create the concept?

Both. We execute agency-approved storyboards faithfully, and we also develop original concepts and scripts for brands who come to us directly.

What is the typical timeline for a commercial?

A standard commercial runs about two to five weeks from brief to final master, depending on casting, locations and the number of deliverables. We lock a milestone schedule before the shoot.

Do you handle casting and locations?

Yes — talent casting, auditions, location scouting across Karachi and permits are all handled as part of the production.

Can you produce commercials outside Karachi?

Yes. We are based in Karachi but shoot nationwide across Pakistan when a campaign calls for it.
