The Look

Images That Carry the Story

Cinematography is not about expensive cameras — it is about light, motion and restraint. The photography on Janjaal helped earn the film 14+ international awards and a Best Trailer win at TMFF Scotland, and that same visual discipline is available to hire for your project.

Cinegma Films provides cinematographer / director of photography services in Karachi — for narrative films, commercials, music videos, documentaries and brand content. We bring the camera package, lighting and the eye; you get frames that feel intentional, not incidental.

Questions & Answers

Hiring a Cinematographer in Karachi

Can I hire Cinegma Films for cinematography only?

Yes. We take on standalone cinematography and DOP engagements — you bring the director and production, we bring the camera, lighting and visual craft. We also provide full production if you need it.

What cameras and gear do you shoot on?

We shoot on professional cinema cameras with prime and zoom lens sets, supported by lighting, grip and movement gear appropriate to the project. The exact package is matched to your look and budget.

Do you provide a full camera crew?

Yes. Depending on scale we coordinate focus pullers, gaffers, grips and camera assistants so the camera department runs smoothly on the day.

Will the footage be ready for colour grading?

Always. We shoot with the grade in mind — correct exposure, monitoring LUTs and log capture where appropriate — so the footage arrives in post ready to be graded, not repaired. We can also grade it for you.

Do you travel outside Karachi for shoots?

Yes, we are based in Karachi and available for cinematography work across Pakistan.
