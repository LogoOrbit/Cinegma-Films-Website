About the film

Portrait of Life, the short film

Portrait of Life is a 2025 Pakistani short film — an 11-minute drama written, directed, edited and colour graded by Syed Asad Raza Abidi, produced by Dayyan Khalid. It stars Umeza Irfan and Zuhair Zubair, with cinematography by Ghayas Uddin Siddiqui and production design by Faizan Ahmed.

A painter struggles to portray his wife the way he once did. Told through a Van Gogh–inflected visual language, Portrait of Life is a quiet meditation on love, memory, and the limits of art — a thesis film completed on 2 June 2025, from the same director as Janjaal.

The film was an Official Selection at the Anymusic Film Festival, Spain, and its trailer was a Best Trailer nominee at The Monthly Film Festival (TMFF), Scotland.
