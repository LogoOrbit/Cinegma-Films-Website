A fisherman survives a deadly boat incident and comes away with a golden pocket watch that grants wishes — at a steep cost. A grieving police officer, desperate to bring his son back, discovers the watch’s dark power. The trailer for Janjaal sets up that bargain in salt, sand and dread, cut to an original score by Hassan Khan.

Edited by director Syed Asad Raza Abidi, the Janjaal trailer won Best Trailer at The Monthly Film Festival (TMFF), Scotland, 2025 — one of the 14 international awards the film has collected across 9 countries.

About the film

  • Title: Janjaal (جنجال) — English title Tussle
  • Type: Pakistani short film · psychological thriller
  • Director: Syed Asad Raza Abidi
  • Starring: Shamoon Abbasi, Nazish Ali, Danish Irshad
  • Runtime: 24 minutes · Urdu
  • Premiere: Arena Cinemas, Karachi — 5 January 2025
