Cast

Janjaal cast

Janjaal is led by Shamoon Abbasi, one of the most familiar faces in Pakistani film and television, as Raza — a police officer who reaches for a cursed golden pocket watch in the hope of undoing his son’s death. He is joined by Nazish Ali as Zareen and Danish Irshad as Azad. As Syed Asad recalls of casting Abbasi: “He read it and said yes. He’s a straightforward person. He responds to the work.”

Crew

Made by these hands

Every member of the core crew of Janjaal was a student. Director Syed Asad Raza Abidi developed the film in his sixth semester of the Film & TV programme at Indus University, Karachi, and personally handled the edit, colour grade, VFX and 5.1 surround mix.

FAQ

Cast & crew questions

Who plays the lead in Janjaal?

Veteran Pakistani actor Shamoon Abbasi leads Janjaal as Raza, a police officer who turns to a cursed golden pocket watch after losing his son.

Who wrote Janjaal?

Janjaal was written by Ameer Hamza with co-writer Basil Hussain Zaidi; the screenplay is credited to Syed Asad and Ameer Hamza.

Who produced Janjaal?

Janjaal was produced by Faizan Ahmed for Cinegma Films, with Dayyan Khalid as associate producer.

Who shot Janjaal?

The cinematographer (DOP) of Janjaal is Ghayas Uddin Siddiqui, with Ammar Bhatti as second-unit DOP. The film was shot on a Sony FX3 in 2.35:1.

Who composed the music for Janjaal?

The original score of Janjaal is by Hassan Khan of Wild Media.
