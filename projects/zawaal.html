About the film

Zawaal, the short film

Zawaal — Urdu for "hour of decline" — is an upcoming Pakistani Urdu-language psychological thriller short film directed and edited by Syed Asad Raza Abidi, produced by Dayyan Khalid, written by Faizan Ahmed with co-writer Ameer Hamza. It stars Ameed Akbar, Ali Buksh, Danish Irshad and Nazish Ali, with cinematography by Ghayas Uddin Siddiqui.

Set in the streets of Karachi, Zawaal follows a man confronting the choices that shaped the ruin of everything he built — a dark, inevitable descent that pushes the boundaries of independent Pakistani cinema.

Zawaal is the next original from the studio behind Janjaal, which won 14 international awards across 9 countries. It is currently in production; the official 4K trailer is out now.
