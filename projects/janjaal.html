JanjaalTussle

2024 – 2025 · Theatrical Release

A fisherman survives a deadly boat incident with a mysterious golden pocket watch that grants wishes, but at a steep cost. As a police officer seeks to revive his son, he discovers the dark power of the watch, leading to devastating consequences. Shot in 34 hours across Karachi, Janjaal travelled across 9 countries and won 14 international awards.

Director's Statement
"Janjaal is a story about grief, desperation, and the dangerous allure of desire. I wanted to explore how far someone can go when faced with unbearable loss, and how even hope can become a curse when tainted by greed. The golden pocket watch symbolizes temptation — a gift that comes with a hidden knife. My goal was to make the audience feel unsettled, to confront them with the question: What would you sacrifice to change fate?"
— Syed Asad Raza Abidi
