About the film

Janjaal, the short film

Janjaal (Urdu: جنجال; English title: Tussle) is a 2024 Pakistani Urdu-language psychological thriller short film directed, edited and colour graded by Syed Asad Raza Abidi and produced by Faizan Ahmed under the banner of Cinegma Films. Written by Ameer Hamza with Basil Hussain Zaidi, it stars veteran actor Shamoon Abbasi, with Nazish Ali and Danish Irshad.

The 24-minute film was shot in just 34 hours, largely on Sunhera Beach on the coastal edge of Karachi, by a crew of film students from Indus University. It premiered theatrically at Arena Cinemas, Karachi on 5 January 2025 — a rare cinema release for a Pakistani student short — and went on to win 14 international awards and 22 official selections across 9 countries, including Best Short Film at the Toronto Fantasy/Sci-Fi Film Festival 2026.

Janjaal is one of the most awarded Pakistani short films in recent international festival history, and it is the film that launched Cinegma Films as an independent production house.

Story & Themes

A wish with a hidden knife

On the edge of the Arabian Sea, a fisherman survives a deadly boat incident and comes away with a mysterious golden pocket watch. The watch grants wishes — but each one is paid for. When Raza, a grieving police officer played by Shamoon Abbasi, turns to it in the hope of bringing his son back, he discovers just how dark its power is, and how far the price reaches.

The film belongs to the old tradition of cautionary wish stories — the kind in which getting exactly what you asked for is the worst thing that can happen. Janjaal sets that idea against a very Pakistani landscape: fishing boats stranded on the sand, an unforgiving coastline, and a family pulled apart by loss.

Themes

  • Grief and desperation — how far a parent will go when faced with unbearable loss.
  • Temptation and greed — the golden watch as a gift that cuts the hand that takes it.
  • Fate and consequence — the film’s central question: what would you sacrifice to change fate?

Director's Statement
"Janjaal is a story about grief, desperation, and the dangerous allure of desire. I wanted to explore how far someone can go when faced with unbearable loss, and how even hope can become a curse when tainted by greed. The golden pocket watch symbolizes temptation — a gift that comes with a hidden knife. My goal was to make the audience feel unsettled, to confront them with the question: What would you sacrifice to change fate?"
— Syed Asad Raza Abidi, director of Janjaal

Making Of

34 hours on the beach

Janjaal was made by students, on a student budget, against a university deadline — with cinema screens already booked for the premiere. The crew arrived at Sunhera Beach before dawn; the tide was supposed to reach the boats by 7 AM, and at 9:30 they were still on dry sand. The water came, the scene was shot, and the whole film was in the can in 34 hours. Syed Asad then directed the post himself: edit, colour grade, VFX and a 5.1 surround mix.

FAQ

Janjaal — questions

What is Janjaal?

Janjaal (English title: Tussle) is a 2024 Pakistani short film — a 24-minute, Urdu-language psychological thriller directed by Syed Asad Raza Abidi and produced by Cinegma Films. A mysterious golden pocket watch grants wishes, but every wish exacts a deadly price. It premiered theatrically at Arena Cinemas, Karachi on 5 January 2025 and has won 14 international awards across 9 countries.

Who directed the Janjaal short film?

Janjaal was directed by Pakistani filmmaker Syed Asad Raza Abidi, who also edited, colour graded, supervised the VFX and mixed the film in 5.1 surround. It was his debut short under his banner Cinegma Films.

Who is in the cast of Janjaal?

Janjaal stars veteran Pakistani actor Shamoon Abbasi as Raza, with Nazish Ali as Zareen and Danish Irshad as Azad, alongside Syed Ahsan, Ameer Hamza, Ali Mehdi Azmi, Qasim Baloch, Shehryar Habib and Muhammad Usman. See the full Janjaal cast and crew.

The official Janjaal trailer is free to watch on this site. The full 24-minute film streams on the Cinegma Films watch page with a screener password, available on request from info@cinegmafilms.com for press, festivals, distributors and viewers.

How long is Janjaal?

Janjaal runs 24 minutes. It is a short film, not a feature or a TV drama.

What language is Janjaal in?

Janjaal is an Urdu-language film from Pakistan.

What does “Janjaal” mean?

“Janjaal” (جنجال) is an Urdu word for an entanglement, a mess or a struggle you cannot easily escape — which is why the film’s English title is Tussle. The name is sometimes spelled “Janjal”.

What awards has Janjaal won?

Janjaal has won 14 international awards and 22 official selections across 9 countries, including Best Short Film at the Toronto Fantasy/Sci-Fi Film Festival 2026, Best Director and Best Film at Cedar Film Festival 2026, Best Director and Best Short Film at the Kolkata International Short Film Festival 2025, Best Short Film at the Pakistan International Film Festival 2025 and Best Trailer at The Monthly Film Festival, Scotland. See all Janjaal awards.

Where and how was Janjaal filmed?

Janjaal was shot in just 34 hours, largely on Sunhera Beach on the coastal outskirts of Karachi, on a Sony FX3 in 2.35:1 — by a crew made up almost entirely of film students from Indus University. Read how Janjaal was made.

When was Janjaal released?

Janjaal was filmed in 2024 and premiered theatrically at Arena Cinemas, Karachi on 5 January 2025, before its international festival run through 2025 and 2026.
