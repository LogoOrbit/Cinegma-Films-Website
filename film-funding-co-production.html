Funding

Back Films That Travel

Our first short, Janjaal, was made with our own money. It went on to win 14 international awards, play at festivals in 9 countries and get a cinema release in Karachi. Now we want to make more films like it, and bigger ones.

That's why we welcome funding from investors, foundations, grant bodies, brands, crowdfunding backers and co-producers, whether you're in Pakistan or anywhere else in the world. We have projects in production, including Zawaal and A Soldier Beside Me, and we're always open to making a film you want to see made.

Every arrangement is put in writing before money changes hands: what the funds cover, how they're reported and what credit and rights each side gets.

Questions & Answers

Film Funding & Co-Production — Common Questions

Can people outside Pakistan fund your films?

Yes. We're open to funding and partners from anywhere in the world, and we've already worked with festivals and audiences across 9 countries.

What projects are you raising funds for?

We have projects in production, including Zawaal and A Soldier Beside Me. Get in touch and we'll share pitch material for the projects that fit your interests.

Can I fund a film about a specific subject?

Yes. Foundations, NGOs and individuals commission films on subjects they care about. We develop and make the film, and agree credits and rights with you.

How is the money accounted for?

Budgets and reporting are agreed before we start. You know what your money covers and get updates as the film is made.

Do you help with grant applications?

Yes. We can partner with you on applications and provide the materials funders usually ask for, such as scripts, budgets, schedules and past work.
