Questions & Answers

VFX & Animation in Karachi — Common Questions

How is VFX work priced?

Per shot for film VFX and per project for motion graphics and explainers, scoped after seeing your material or brief. Email info@cinegmafilms.com for a shot-by-shot quote.

Can you fix or clean up footage from a shoot?

Often yes — wire and object removal, stabilisation, screen replacements and continuity fixes are routine; send the shot and we'll confirm feasibility honestly.

Do you make animated explainer videos with voiceover?

Yes — script, storyboard, illustration, animation, voiceover and music, delivered as a complete explainer in English and Urdu.

Will motion graphics match our brand guidelines?

Strictly — we animate inside your fonts, colours and logo rules, and provide style frames for approval before full animation.

Do you take remote VFX work from other studios?

Yes — we support agencies and production houses as a white-label VFX and motion unit with clear versioning and delivery specs.
