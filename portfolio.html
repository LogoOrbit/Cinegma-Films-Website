Why It Matters

A Portfolio Built on Range, Not Repetition

From the award-winning psychological thriller Janjaal (Tussle) to the tender drama of Portrait of Life and the observational documentary Urdu Bazaar Karachi, the Cinegma Films portfolio shows a studio that can shift register without losing craft. Every title here was directed, shot and finished by the same core team.

That range is the point. A brand hiring a film production company in Karachi wants proof that one team can handle drama, documentary and commercial work to the same standard — and this is that proof. Explore the individual case studies above, then tell us what you want to make.
